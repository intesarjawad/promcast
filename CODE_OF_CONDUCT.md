# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Critique ideas and code constructively. Respect
privacy and differing experiences. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
attention and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project discussions, contributions and project events.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or exclude
participants. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of conduct.

A private reporting channel has not been configured because repository ownership
and hosting are pending. Do not post private reports in public issues. Establishing
a confidential contact is required before opening community participation.
