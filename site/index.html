Every series is a train on one clock

Promcast predicts db-01 /var/lib/postgresql crosses in 1h 15m. predict_linear says 2h 00m, 45m later, because it extends the last hour's slope and the disk is not filling in a straight line.

Time runs across the sheet and free space runs up it, on a log scale so the last few percent get room. Promcast sends nine quantile paths out of now; predict_linear sends one ruled line. A train stops where it meets the red rule, which marks its predicted crossing.

logs-01 is the honest loss: the ruler gets there 40m first. ingest-02 has a linear crossing prediction at 16:40, while Promcast predicts no crossing.

Runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded.

On your own series

uvx --torch-backend cpu promcast scan
Ranks every disk, volume and memory series by when it runs out.
uvx --torch-backend cpu promcast scan --redact
Replaces host and mount labels with aliases.
uvx --torch-backend cpu promcast replay --auto
Grades alerts against your own history before you trust them.
