Every series is a train on one clock

Promcast predicts {{name}} crosses in {{lead}}. predict_linear says {{linear}}, {{gap}} later, because it extends the last hour's slope and the disk is not filling in a straight line.

Time runs across the sheet and free space runs up it, on a log scale so the last few percent get room. Promcast sends nine quantile paths out of now; predict_linear sends one ruled line. A train stops where it meets the red rule, which marks its predicted crossing.

{{loss}} is the honest loss: the ruler gets there {{loss_gap}} first. {{spare}} has a linear crossing prediction at {{spare_at}}, while Promcast predicts no crossing.

Runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded.
